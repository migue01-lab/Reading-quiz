Reading Comprehension

Elena’s Working Day:
Elena is a graphic designer who works from home three days a week. She enjoys drinking green tea every morning before starting her work at 8:30 AM. Although her company offers free lunch at the main office, Elena prefers cooking her own meals. She usually goes for a 20-minute jog in the evening to relax.

Read each statement and select TRUE, FALSE, or NOT GIVEN.

1. Elena travels to the office every single day.
Explanation: The text says she works from home three days a week, so she does not go to the office every day.
2. Elena drinks coffee while she designs.
Explanation: The passage says she drinks green tea before starting, but it never mentions whether she drinks coffee while working.
3. Elena likes preparing her own food.
Explanation: The text states that "Elena prefers cooking her own meals."
4. Elena runs for half an hour every evening.
Explanation: The passage specifies a "20-minute jog", not half an hour (30 minutes).
